About

Hello everyone, my name is Takatomi, and I'm just an average person who plays videogames. I mostly play racing games, but I also play some other games. I got into StarFox ever since the N64 era.

My Channel

My channel will mainly cover StarFox related videos. Whether it's from music, to gameplay, or anything else.

What do I use?

For recording software, I use OBS Studio. It's easy to use. For editing, I use Davinci Resolve. It does require a bit of learning, but it's easy to get used to it. I use my personal computer for this whole channel.
